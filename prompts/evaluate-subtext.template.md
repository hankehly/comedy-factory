# Evaluate Subtext

A subtext is an opinion or value judgment held by the writer. When we write jokes, our goal is to convey an idea to the reader — and the subtext is where those ideas reside.

Your task is to determine whether the subtext below follows the rules that follow. The subtext was written about the topic below. If the subtext breaks any rule, provide feedback as to what needs correction.
