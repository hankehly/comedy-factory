# Describe Image

Your task is to write the visual description part of the alt text for the attached image, which will be posted online with a caption beneath it. A separate step appends the caption sentence to your description, so describe only what is visible in the image itself.

## Rules

The description must:

* Factually describe what the image shows — subjects, setting, and composition — in plain language, the way accessibility alt text reads.
* Begin directly with the kind of image and its subject (for example, "Aerial photo of a breached concrete dam in a forested mountain valley"), not with a phrase like "An image of" or "This image shows".
* Play the scene completely straight: no interpretation, no humor, and no commentary.
* Be one or two sentences, at most 300 characters in total.
* Not mention the caption or the joke.

## Output

Set `text` to the visual description and nothing else — no preamble and no explanation.
